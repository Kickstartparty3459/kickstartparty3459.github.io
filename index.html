🎯 What Is dsh-ios?

dsh-ios is a powerful plugin that brings a live iOS Simulator — or a USB-connected iPhone — directly into your conversation window. It's like having a real iPhone sitting next to you, ready to test apps, explore interfaces, and automate tasks, all without leaving your chat.

Whether you're a developer testing your latest app, a QA specialist checking user flows, or just curious about iOS automation, dsh-ios gives you 22 powerful tools to control your device with simple commands.

⚡ Key Features

🖥️ Live Simulator Control

Boot, build, and interact with iOS Simulators in real-time. See every change instantly.

📱 Real iPhone Connection

Connect your USB iPhone and control it directly from your conversation.

🔍 Smart UI Recognition

Drive the interface by accessibility identity, OCR text, or list rows — no complex selectors needed.

🔄 Streaming Sidebar

A live sidebar panel you can tap and drag, showing real-time updates as you work.

🔧 How It Works

dsh-ios works as a plugin within the DeepSeek Harness (DSH) ecosystem. It uses powerful automation tools like WebDriverAgent to communicate with your iOS devices. The plugin provides a simple, conversational interface to control complex iOS interactions.
